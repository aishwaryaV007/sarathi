# Sarathi 🧭

**Your plain-language guide to business approvals and compliance in India.**

*Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem Statement 26130: Efficiency in streamlining industrial approvals, compliance processes, and access to government support services)*

---

## 🚀 The Problem

When an entrepreneur starts a business in India, they need 8–15 different government approvals across various departments. Finding out *exactly* which ones apply to their specific business, what documents are needed, and where to apply is confusing and often requires paying expensive liaison agents.

Existing government portals act as **submission counters**—you must already know what to apply for.

## ✨ Our Solution: Sarathi

Sarathi is an intelligent web application that acts as a guide, not just a counter. It generates a customized approval checklist, guides documentation, pre-validates submissions, reuses verified data, and tracks everything in one dashboard.

### Key Features
- **Plain-Language Understanding**: Users describe their business in their own words, and Sarathi understands the requirements.
- **Statute-Cited Output**: Every required approval cites the exact law that mandates it. Verifiable and never a guess.
- **One-Place Application & Document Vault**: Upload documents (PAN/Aadhaar) once, and reuse them across all applications.
- **Universal Support**: Works for ANY business type, from a small kirana store to a private limited manufacturing factory.
- **100% Free**: Democratizing compliance for first-time entrepreneurs.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript 5
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui, base-ui
- **Backend & Database**: Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, pgvector)
- **AI Layer**: Vercel AI SDK with Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) for natural language understanding (with local keyword fallback)
- **Validation**: Zod

> **Note on AI**: The LLM is used *strictly* to translate the user's sentence into structured facts. The actual approval checklist is generated by a deterministic rules engine and a curated dataset to ensure 100% accuracy and avoid hallucinations.

## ⚙️ Architecture & Flow

1. **Describe**: User types their business idea and answers a few clarifying questions.
2. **Understand**: LLM (or keyword fallback) extracts structured facts (e.g., investment size, workers, sector).
3. **Classify & Assemble**: The internal rules engine calculates MSME category, pollution category, and aggregates required universal and sector-specific approvals.
4. **Action**: User views the checklist, uploads documents to their secure vault, and applies for licenses directly through pre-filled forms.

First, install the dependencies if you haven't already:


```bash

# or
```
